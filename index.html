02
Singlife · Propel

Designing products with users, not for users.

As the sole product designer on the rebuild of a legacy financial-advisory platform, I moved the process away from designing screens around requirements and toward understanding how advisers actually work.

PROPEL
How the process changed
01

Real scenarios first

Start with actual adviser situations rather than showing screens and asking users what they think.

02

Co-create before designing

Use advisers and managers to surface behaviours, workarounds, constraints and unmet needs before committing to a solution.

03

Translate behaviour into systems

Turn discovery into journey maps, information architecture, interaction models and product concepts.

04

Prototype and test

Rapidly test assumptions and observe how advisers interpret modules, terminology, actions and information.

05

Build reusable foundations

Establish the product's design language and reusable system while continuously challenging feature requests against the underlying problem.

04 About

I’m interested in what happens when design moves beyond the interface.

I’m a Lead Product Designer with 8+ years across enterprise products, financial services, startup platforms and large-scale experiences.

I work best in complex, ambiguous environments: understanding what is actually happening, connecting user and business needs, and turning that complexity into systems that people can use and organisations can deliver.

My work now sits across product, systems, strategy, technology and experience — because the problems I’m interested in rarely stop at the edge of a screen.
